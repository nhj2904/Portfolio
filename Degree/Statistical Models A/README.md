# Statistical Models A Projects

Projects completed for the F79MA Statistical Models A course during my Year 2 studies at Heriot-Watt University Malaysia.

| Project | Date | Languages Used | About the Project | What I Did |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maximum Likelihood Estimation and Simulation | October 2024 | R | Investigated the performance of a maximum likelihood estimator for a probability distribution introduced in an insurance modelling scenario. Mathematical derivations and simulations were used to analyse estimation accuracy across different sample sizes. | Derived statistical estimators and theoretical measures, developed R simulations, analysed bias, variance and mean squared error, and visualised simulation results. |
| Bayesian Lifetime Modelling and Predictive Simulation | November 2024 | R | Analysed the lifetime of drilling machine components using the Maxwell distribution in a semiconductor manufacturing scenario. Classical and Bayesian estimation methods were applied alongside predictive modelling to assess reliability. | Derived Bayesian estimators, implemented simulation-based analysis in R, evaluated estimator performance, and developed predictive reliability analysis. |
